# Uniland · Pearl Launch
## Style Prompt
Apple-inspired product launch minimalism: spacious pearl-white canvas, quiet graphite typography, soft silver architecture, translucent cards, precise blue accents, restrained cinematic depth. Original vehicle photographs must retain their proportions and identity.
## Colors
- #F5F5F7 pearl canvas
- #FFFFFF glass surface
- #1D1D1F primary ink
- #626773 secondary ink
- #245BB2 Uniland blue
## Typography
Arial / Helvetica Neue, clean sans serif; large light-weight headlines, tabular data.
## Motion
Slow perspective entrances, staggered fade and lift, title light sweeps, soft number glow. No bounce. Car layers use contain and slow transform-only parallax when source photos are supplied.
## What NOT to Do
No invented vehicle imagery. No stretching. No fabricated claims. No clutter, saturated backgrounds, or aggressive motion.
