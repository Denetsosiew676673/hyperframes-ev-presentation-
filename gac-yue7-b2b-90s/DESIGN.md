## Style Prompt
A restrained automotive export film in the language of a premium product launch. Charcoal architectural space, matte silver typography, pale cyan technical drafting. Authentic supplied photographs retain their original vehicle colour. Large negative space and precise diagrams; no invented capability footage.
## Colors
- #101519: charcoal canvas
- #192126: raised technical surfaces
- #F3F5F5: primary white
- #AFBABF: matte silver secondary
- #A7DCE6: pale cyan parameters and traced geometry
## Typography
Helvetica Neue / Arial, 700 for titles and captions, 400 for supporting technical labels. Tabular numbers.
## Motion
Slow 0.7–1.2 second entrances; linear photographic moves under 4%; 0.8 second dissolves. Parameter lift 12px, 0.55s fade in, hold 2s, 0.55s fade out. No overshoot.
## What NOT to Do
No saturated neon, stickers, cartoon illustration, lens flares, fast cuts, shaky camera or bouncing typography. No Chinese text. No text on vehicle body.
